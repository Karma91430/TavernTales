Battles you can see

Your party faces the enemy on a live battlefield: turn order, HP rings, conditions, floating damage and a clear set of actions.

  • Attack, cast, or use a skill move: Shove, Feint, Hide, Study Weakness
  • Companions and enemies act on their own turns

“How do you want to do this?”

Land the killing blow on a boss and the game slows down to let you describe it. Pick a suggestion or write your own; the narrator makes it the highlight of the fight.

Create your legend

Six classes, six races and rolled ability scores, then a premise and a tone. Or hit Surprise me and start playing right away.

Characters that grow

Real choices every level:

  • Level 2: a subclass such as Assassin, Battle Master or Life Domain
  • Level 3: a new ability and a new skill
  • Level 4: ability scores or a feat

Moments, not menus

Resting at camp, leveling up, completing a quest, entering a new place or facing a boss each gets its own animated moment. Camps in the wild can be ambushed.

Plus Iron & Ash for grim tales and High Seas for swashbuckling ones.

Three models, one table

Instead of one chatbot doing everything badly, each job goes to the right model. A TypeScript rules engine sits in the middle and keeps everyone honest.

Get started

Three steps, no account.

  • 🦙Ollama installed and running (download).
  • 🧠Models: qwen3:8b and qwen3.5:2b by default. Any chat model works; pick them in Settings.
  • 🟢Node.js 22 or newer (the repo's .nvmrc pins Node 26).
  • 💻Hardware: a machine that runs an 8B model comfortably, e.g. a recent Mac or a GPU with 8 GB of VRAM.
# 1. get the code
$ git clone https://github.com/Karma91430/TavernTales.git
$ cd TavernTales

# 2. get the models
$ ollama pull qwen3:8b
$ ollama pull qwen3.5:2b

# 3. play
$ npm install
$ npm run dev   # → http://localhost:5173

Questions

Does it need the internet?

Only to download it and the models. After that the game, the AI and your saves all stay on your machine.

Is it real Dungeons & Dragons?

It's inspired by the 5th edition rules (ability checks, AC, advantage, spell slots, subclasses), simplified to fit a solo game with AI companions. It isn't an official D&D product.

Can I use other models?

Yes. Any Ollama chat model works, and each role can use a different one. A bigger narrator writes better prose; a small, fast arbiter keeps turns snappy.

How long is a turn?

On a laptop running an 8B model, about 20 to 40 seconds, and the narration streams in as it's written. Turn on fast dice in Settings to speed up combat.
